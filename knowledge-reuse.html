Knowledge Reuse and the Renewal of Innovation

Chenhao Zhou · Working paper · Target: Management Science

This study examines how access to existing technical capability changes repeated research decisions. It distinguishes developers’ incentives from the benefits that applications deliver to users.

The project connects research on technology and coordination with innovation competition and the renewal of technical knowledge.

For the current manuscript, please contact chenhao.zhou@rutgers.edu.

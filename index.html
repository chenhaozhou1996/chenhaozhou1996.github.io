About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Management, with a concentration in Supply Chain Management, and a Graduate Instructor at Rutgers Business School. My dissertation committee includes Weiwei Chen, Xin Ding, and Keith Skowronski, and is chaired by David Dreyfus.

My current research examines how public policy and environmental conditions—both shifts and persistent differences—affect organizational performance, and how firms adapt operations strategy across institutional contingencies.

Research interests: healthcare operations; supply chain relationships & sustainability. Methods: causal identification & structural estimation.
